# E1: hmdposer (CMU + Xsens train, commercial-clean) vs Basis

Oracle-v2 (tag oracle-v2), inputs-v1, splits-v1, 60 Hz, cm, jitter in 1e2 m/s^3 (GT in brackets). E1 = HMD-Poser-design
(`train.py`, model `hmdposer`) trained on CMU train (+mirrors) and Xsens train minus S09, causal 40-frame windows, 35k+15k
steps (2.6 GPU-h), checkpoint = best on val (step 10 000 of 50 000, selected on mean val MPJPE-full of the quick-val
subsample). Rotation outputs are placed with the clip GT bone lengths (oracle-v2 (a)); Basis rows are identical to
oracle-v1. The test table is the one-time run of the selected checkpoint. Note: xsens_eval shares subjects S01, S02, S05
with train (the named clips have unknown subject), so it flatters E1; xsens_val (S09) is the held-out subject.
GT reference (own values, not zero by definition): skate 2.8 (CMU) / 4.7 (Xsens eval) / 5.5 (S09) cm/s, float p95 4.3-4.8 cm.
Later checkpoints of the same run were worse on S09 (last.pt, step 50 000: upper 6.92, lower 19.57, jitter 3.28), and train
error keeps falling (Xsens train subset upper 1.4 cm in quick-val): overfitting to the 8 Xsens training subjects.
